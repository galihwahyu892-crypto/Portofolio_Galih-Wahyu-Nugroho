P-01

Electric Boat-Tractor Prototype

ITS, STP Otomotif and the Ministry of Agriculture RI · Mar – Jun 2026 · Team of four students (PjBL 4-30)

FIPEC 2026 · Most Innovative
Problem

Boat tractors for muddy rice fields and shallow water still run on diesel. The Agropro FS-28E, for example, uses a 25 HP diesel at up to 2,200 rpm. High load at low speed means heavy maintenance, noise and fuel use.

Solution

An electric propulsion system: a 10 kW PMSM motor and controller fed by a 72 V 140 Ah LiFePO4 battery with BMS, with CAN-based monitoring and protection.

My part
  • Project finance: recorded income and expenses, prepared financial reports and kept transaction records traceable.
  • Cash flow: prepared cash-flow statements, tracked spending against available funds and identified the funding each activity needed.
  • Project administration: kept daily progress records and compiled the project report.
  • Technical: drew the system block diagram and installed the motor controller (team work breakdown).
  • BMS team: worked with the technical team on LiFePO4 battery and BMS integration.

P-03

PID Speed Control & Sorting Conveyor

Control system design · ATmega328P · MATLAB/Simulink · group project

Problem

Motor speed drifts when the load changes, which makes the process less stable and hurts conveyor performance.

Approach

PID was modelled and simulated in MATLAB/Simulink, then PI/PID ran on an ATmega328P with tachometer feedback for DC and AC motor speed control.

Result

A working automatic conveyor with closed-loop speed control and metal and non-metal sorting using an inductive sensor and a servo. Testing showed stable speed with a small steady-state error.

My part

Designed the AC motor plant and the VFD integration with the ATmega328P system, collected motor data and helped assemble the conveyor.

P-04

Fluid Mixing with Ratio Control

Process control · MATLAB/Simulink, OpenPLC and ScadaBR · Modbus TCP/IP

Problem

A wrong base-to-pigment ratio changes the final colour, the viscosity and the product quality.

Approach

The P&ID was designed, the plant modelled in MATLAB/Simulink, then PI ratio control ran on OpenPLC over Modbus TCP/IP. FT-101 and FT-102 measure the two flows, and FCV-102 regulates pigment flow to a 4:1 (0.25) setpoint.

Result

At a base flow of 400 L/min the pigment flow held at 100 L/min. Tuning at Kp = 3 and Ki = 0.5 gave a smooth response with minimal overshoot and settling under 20 s.

My part

Designed and simulated the PI ratio control system in MATLAB/Simulink and OpenPLC.
